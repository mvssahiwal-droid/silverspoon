OUR STORY

A Taste of Tradition

Silver Spoon Restaurant was born from a passion for authentic South Asian cuisine and a desire to share it with Vienna.

Our journey began in family kitchens where traditional recipes were perfected over generations. Now, we bring these cherished flavors to Josef-Palme Platz, combining time-honored cooking techniques with fresh, high-quality ingredients.

Every dish tells a story - from our aromatic biryanis simmered for hours to our creamy butter chicken prepared with homemade spice blends.

OUR MISSION

To provide Vienna with authentic Pakistani and Indian cuisine that stays true to traditional recipes while embracing quality and consistency.

  • Quality
  • Hospitality

MEET OUR CHEF

With over 40 years of experience in Pakistani and Indian kitchens, our head chef brings both tradition and innovation to every dish. Trained in Lahore and Delhi, he ensures each recipe maintains its authentic roots while appealing to Vienna's diverse palate.

"Food is more than sustenance - it's a connection to culture, family, and memory. At Silver Spoon, we serve not just meals, but experiences."
- Chef Mohammad Azam
